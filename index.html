01 — ABOUT

Building systems that move data with intent.

I’m Omkar Rikke, a data engineer focused on end-to-end pipelines: ingestion, orchestration, transformation, and serving analytics. My work lives at the intersection of batch and streaming — Airflow DAGs, Kafka topics, Delta Lake layers, and warehouse models that analysts can actually trust.

Recent work includes a weekly US gas-price tracker on Airflow + Databricks (bronze / silver / gold), a real-time stock market pipeline on Kafka and AWS, flight-ops telemetry into Snowflake, and an Azure Olympics pipeline spanning Data Factory, Databricks, and Synapse.

I care about idempotent loads, observable DAGs, and documentation that survives the next on-call. Always happy to talk pipelines, lakehouse design, or the next dataset worth modeling.
